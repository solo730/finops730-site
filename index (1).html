Why this exists

Built for the stage where a FinOps hire doesn't pencil out yet

Most early-stage SaaS teams don't have a cost problem because they're careless — they have one because nobody's job is to watch the bill. Engineering ships, spend creeps, and Activate credits expire quietly in the background. FinOps730 is that job, sized for a startup budget.

01 — Audit

A 10-point pass across your AWS account

Rightsizing, unattached storage, region sprawl, IAM hygiene, tagging, and credit expiry — flagged by dollar impact, not noise.

02 — Advise

A plan, not a dashboard

Recommendations you or your team can execute, prioritized by savings and effort. We never touch your infrastructure directly.

03 — Monitor

Someone watching the bill weekday mornings

Daily monitoring on the Advisory + Monitoring tier means drift gets caught before it becomes next month's surprise invoice.
