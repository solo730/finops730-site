About

Run by one person, on purpose

FinOps730 LLC is a solo advisory practice. No account managers, no handoffs — the person who runs your audit is the person you talk to.

Stephen Matthews

Founder of FinOps730 LLC. Background in hands-on AWS architecture — serverless systems built on Lambda, DynamoDB, API Gateway, S3/CloudFront, Cognito, and SES — combined with a FinOps Certified Practitioner credential focused specifically on cloud cost management.

FinOps730 exists for a specific gap: startups past the free-credits honeymoon but too early to justify a full-time cost hire. The audit process and monitoring cadence are built around that stage, not enterprise cloud governance.
